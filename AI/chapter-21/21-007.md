# AIP architecture / AIP 架构

As described in the [overview of AIP, Foundry, and Apollo](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/platforms/), AIP is Palantir’s platform for connecting generative AI into operational domains. While AIP and Foundry operate together as part of a shared service mesh, powered by Apollo and deployed within [Rubix](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/rubix/), this page provides a view of AIP's end-to-end architecture through an AI-centric lens. This includes capabilities for securely connecting to the full range of LLMs, continuously integrating context into the Ontology, building agents and automations, observing and evaluating the ongoing performance of deployed agents, and a full developer toolchain for managing AI-driven products.

正如 [AIP、Foundry 与 Apollo 概述](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/platforms/)中所述，AIP 是 Palantir 将生成式 AI 接入运营领域的平台。AIP 和 Foundry 由 Apollo 提供支持，并部署在 [Rubix](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/rubix/) 中，作为共享服务网格的一部分协同运行；本文则从以 AI 为中心的视角介绍 AIP 的端到端架构。其中包括安全连接各类 LLM、持续将上下文集成到 Ontology、构建智能体与自动化流程、观察和评估已部署智能体的持续表现，以及用于管理 AI 驱动产品的完整开发者工具链。

The AIP architecture can be summarized into 12 general categories of capability:

AIP 架构可以概括为以下 12 类通用能力：

![Diagram of AIP architecture, including the 12 general categories of capability. / AIP 架构图，包括 12 类通用能力。](/static/images/2609/p043.png)

1. **Secure LLM integration & access:** Enabling secure access to the full range of commercial LLMs (e.g., GPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok models) and open-source models (e.g., Llama), through Palantir-managed infrastructure that ensures that no transmitted data is retained by third-party providers, and no transmitted data is used for retraining by model providers. Enterprises can also integrate their existing models, whether existing model subscriptions, fine-tuned models, or domain-specific models.

   **安全的 LLM 集成与访问：**通过 Palantir 管理的基础设施，安全访问各种商业 LLM（例如 GPT、Gemini、Claude、Grok）和开源模型（例如 Llama）。该基础设施确保第三方供应商不会保留任何传输的数据，模型供应商也不会使用这些数据进行再训练。企业还可以集成自己的现有模型，包括已订阅的模型、微调模型和特定领域模型。

2. **End-to-end observability:** Providing monitoring tools for every step of AI-driven workflows and agentic processes. This includes fine-grained monitoring for all data flows that feed the Ontology, logging for every action taken by human users or AI agents, and the ability to trace the cascade of chained executions in a workflow. This observability extends to token consumption and other aspects of resource usage.

   **端到端可观测性：**为 AI 驱动工作流和智能体流程的每个步骤提供监控工具，包括对所有流入 Ontology 的数据流进行细粒度监控，记录人类用户或 AI 智能体执行的每项操作，并追踪工作流中级联的链式执行过程。这种可观测性还覆盖 Token 消耗及资源使用的其他方面。

3. **Context engineering:** Equipping developers with no-, low-, and pro-code tools for integrating the contextual data, logic, action that power the Ontology and all dependent workflows. All modalities of data integration (e.g., batch, streaming, real-time replication via CDC) can be leveraged through any runtime (e.g., Spark, Flink, DataFusion, Polars), while adhering to cohesive security, governance, provenance-tracking, and other essential guarantees.

   **上下文工程：**为开发者提供无代码、低代码和专业代码工具，以集成驱动 Ontology 及其所有依赖工作流的上下文数据、逻辑和操作。各种数据集成模式（例如批处理、流处理、通过 CDC 实时复制）都可以使用任意运行时（例如 Spark、Flink、DataFusion、Polars），同时遵循一致的安全、治理、来源追踪及其他关键保障要求。

4. **The Ontology system:** Activating context by integrating disparate data, logic, action, and security into a unified representation of enterprise decision-making. [Read more about the Ontology.](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/ontology-system/)

   **Ontology 系统：**通过将分散的数据、逻辑、操作和安全机制整合为企业决策的统一表示来激活上下文。[进一步了解 Ontology。](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/ontology-system/)

   * The Ontology’s language models the "nouns" and "verbs" of operational processes into a legible form for both humans and agents.
     
     Ontology 的语言将运营流程中的“名词”和“动词”建模为人类和智能体都能理解的形式。

   * The Ontology’s engine enables querying billions of objects, orchestrating tens-of-thousands of actions, and continuously incorporating feedback-based learning.
     
     Ontology 的引擎支持查询数十亿个对象、编排数万项操作，并持续融入基于反馈的学习成果。

   * The Ontology’s toolchain empowers developers to build diverse and complex AI-powered applications, atop a common foundation.
     
     Ontology 的工具链使开发者能够在统一基础之上构建多样且复杂的 AI 驱动应用。

5. **Vector, compute, tool services:** Providing the integrated vectorization services needed to produce and manage embeddings; an extensible compute framework that can leverage multi-node engines (e.g., Spark, Flink), efficient single-node engines (e.g., DuckDB, Polars), and any containerized “BYO” engine; and an integrated set of tool services that work with the Ontology system to function as an ever-evolving tool factory. The platform is designed to be modular and extensible with respect to models, compute engines, and interfaces.

   **向量、计算与工具服务：**提供生成和管理嵌入向量所需的集成向量化服务；提供可扩展计算框架，支持 Spark、Flink 等多节点引擎，DuckDB、Polars 等高效单节点引擎，以及任意容器化的“自带”引擎；还提供一组与 Ontology 系统协作的集成工具服务，使其成为持续演进的工具工厂。平台在模型、计算引擎和接口方面均按模块化、可扩展方式设计。

6. **Security & governance:** Ensuring that every operation made by humans and agents abides by rigorous role-, marking-, and purpose-based controls. This requires a combination of infrastructure, platform, and enterprise security controls. These controls can be granularly configured and dynamically interrogated, and are cataloged in expressive audit logging. Governance capabilities extend uniformly across all operational, engineering, and developer activities performed within the platform interfaces as well as programmatically through the APIs/SDKs.

   **安全与治理：**确保人类和智能体执行的每项操作都遵循严格的基于角色、数据标记和使用目的的控制要求。这需要基础设施、平台和企业安全控制相互结合。这些控制可以进行细粒度配置和动态查询，并记录在信息丰富的审计日志中。治理能力统一覆盖通过平台界面执行的所有运营、工程和开发活动，以及通过 API/SDK 以编程方式执行的活动。

7. **Agent lifecycle:** Powering the interconnected building, orchestration, and evaluation processes for agents in production. Agents can be constructed using no-, low-, and pro-code workbenches. Durable orchestrations can be configured and managed through low-code interfaces like [AIP Logic](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/logic/overview/), or pro-code interfaces like [Code Workspaces](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/code-workspaces/overview/). The integrated evaluation framework ([AIP Evals](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip-evals/overview/)) operates seamlessly with the Ontology, enabling you to create test cases, debug and iterate on agent definitions, compare performance across different LLMs, examine the variance across executions, and more.

   **智能体生命周期：**支持生产环境中智能体彼此关联的构建、编排和评估流程。智能体可通过无代码、低代码和专业代码工作台构建。持久化编排既可通过 [AIP Logic](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/logic/overview/) 等低代码界面配置和管理，也可通过 [Code Workspaces](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/code-workspaces/overview/) 等专业代码界面完成。集成评估框架 [AIP Evals](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip-evals/overview/) 与 Ontology 无缝协作，支持创建测试用例、调试并迭代智能体定义、比较不同 LLM 的表现、分析多次执行之间的差异等。

8. **Operational automation:** Facilitating the different modes of automation required within and across workflows. This includes scalable schedule-based automations, near real-time event-driven automations that process streaming data, and automations which are enmeshed with API-driven operations. Regardless of the modality, every automation can leverage the rich set of data, logic, and action primitives in the Ontology system, and a wide array of execution and notification configurations.

   **运营自动化：**支持工作流内部和跨工作流所需的不同自动化模式，包括可扩展的定时自动化、处理流式数据的近实时事件驱动自动化，以及与 API 驱动操作紧密结合的自动化。无论采用何种模式，每个自动化流程都可以利用 Ontology 系统中丰富的数据、逻辑和操作原语，以及多样的执行与通知配置。

9. **Development environments:** Empowering developers to build agents and automations, how and where they want to. AIP provides integrated development environments (e.g., VS Code, JupyterLab), which provide seamless connectivity with Ontology-driven applications and integrated testing and evaluation frameworks. In equal measure, the [Platform SDK ↗](https://github.com/palantir/foundry-platform-python) and [Ontology SDK](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ontology-sdk/overview/), in conjunction with [Palantir’s VS Code plug-in](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/palantir-extension-for-visual-studio-code/overview/), bring the same core functionality to existing environments and developer toolchains. Additionally, [Palantir MCP](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/palantir-mcp/overview/) provides a secure interface for agentic development (analogous to what is possible in the platform with AI FDE).

   **开发环境：**使开发者能够按照自己希望的方式和地点构建智能体与自动化。AIP 提供 VS Code、JupyterLab 等集成开发环境，可与 Ontology 驱动的应用及集成测试和评估框架无缝连接。同样，[Platform SDK ↗](https://github.com/palantir/foundry-platform-python) 和 [Ontology SDK](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ontology-sdk/overview/) 配合 [Palantir 的 VS Code 插件](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/palantir-extension-for-visual-studio-code/overview/)，可将相同的核心功能带入现有环境和开发者工具链。此外，[Palantir MCP](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/palantir-mcp/overview/) 还为智能体开发提供安全接口，其能力类似于平台内 AI FDE 所能实现的功能。

10. **Human + AI applications:** Providing the full spectrum of AI-driven experiences; from object-oriented analytics, to real-time application building, to multimodal governance workflows, to the administration of core platform capabilities. Operational users, compliance teams, engineers, analysts, and other key personas have out-of-the-box applications specifically tailored for their workflows, as well as the ability to rapidly construct new Ontology-powered applications. In each case, the infusion of AI can be carefully controlled and transparently assessed, ensuring a smooth journey from augmentation to automation.

    **人类 + AI 应用：**提供全方位的 AI 驱动体验，包括面向对象的分析、实时应用构建、多模态治理工作流以及核心平台能力管理。运营用户、合规团队、工程师、分析师和其他关键角色既可以使用专门针对其工作流定制的开箱即用应用，也能快速构建由 Ontology 驱动的新应用。在各种情况下，AI 的引入都可以得到审慎控制和透明评估，从而确保从辅助增强到自动化的平稳过渡。

11. **Package, release, deploy:** Allowing developers to move beyond point analytics and applications to build fully featured AI products which leverage an integrated DevOps toolchain. End-to-end collections of data pipelines, Ontology definitions, automations, and prebuilt applications can be packaged, released, and deployed across heterogeneous target environments. Developers can specify allowances for "last-mile" customization, and downstream teams can securely receive updates as product definitions evolve, and as changes are validated and promoted across release channels.

    **打包、发布与部署：**使开发者能够超越单点分析和应用，利用集成 DevOps 工具链构建功能完整的 AI 产品。由数据管道、Ontology 定义、自动化和预构建应用组成的端到端集合，可以跨异构目标环境进行打包、发布和部署。开发者可以指定“最后一公里”的定制空间；随着产品定义演进，以及变更通过验证并在各发布渠道中逐步推广，下游团队可以安全地接收更新。

12. **Enterprise automation:** Enabling builders, of all personas and backgrounds, to wield specialized AI agents (e.g., [AI FDE](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ai-fde/overview/), [AIP Analyst](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip-analyst/overview/)) to construct data pipelines, write business logic, train models, build ontologies, produce analytics, and develop end-to-end applications. Critically, these agents operate atop the same foundation as human users, meaning that they abide by the same integrated change management capabilities (e.g., Global Branching), and can seamlessly weave human-in-the-loop workflows with entirely autonomous operations.

    **企业自动化：**使各种角色和背景的构建者都能运用专用 AI 智能体，例如 [AI FDE](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ai-fde/overview/) 和 [AIP Analyst](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip-analyst/overview/)，构建数据管道、编写业务逻辑、训练模型、构建 Ontology、生成分析并开发端到端应用。关键在于，这些智能体与人类用户运行在相同基础之上，因此遵循相同的集成变更管理能力（例如 Global Branching），并能将人在回路工作流与完全自主的操作无缝结合。
